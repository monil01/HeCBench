# Source provenance

The propagation workers derive from EasyWave kernels.cuh by Andrey Babeyko and Johannes Spazier (2014), GFZ German Research Centre for Geosciences. The original source and EUPL1.1 notice, including the scientific-source-availability provision, remain in ../easyWave-cuda/easywave.h and the original source tree. The complete modified Julia workers and study drivers are retained here for scientific reproducibility.
