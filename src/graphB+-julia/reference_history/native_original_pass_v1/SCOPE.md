All numerical comparisons are exact (atol=rtol=0). Independent CPU BFS/hash max-parent selection, subtree/potential/DSU/quotient-distance algorithms check every tree and complete directed-edge/node count. Full raw CSR/interval/worklist states and guards are retained; only order with an explicitly checked bijection may be normalized. Raw atomic queue/worklist identity is not claimed.
