\paragraph{Elimination time is not complete solution time} The selected n128 timer contains the fan1/fan2 forward-elimination loop. Host back substitution and scaled-residual checking occur after elapsed time is recorded. The kernel family therefore describes elimination, although the numerical evidence also checks the final solution; this does not establish a full-solve roofline rate.

\paragraph{Training contains resets and diagnostics as well as arithmetic} The sparse logistic-regression timer includes32 compute/update iterations and per-iteration gradient/objective/accuracy/l2 resets. The update kernel also reduces the old-weight norm. A compute/update source-family match does not assign other fill kernels or establish a complete sampled training-phase census; those observations remain explicitly unassigned.

\paragraph{Selected branch and precision are separate facts} The captured GELU command selects vec2; baseline branches present in source are excluded from this configuration. Its source rounds the bias addition through Float16 and evaluates the tanh polynomial with Float32 intermediates before half projection. GeGLU instead uses an erf gate, and GLU a sigmoid gate. Similar activation labels or half storage do not establish equal executed work, precision, or causal bottlenecks.
