\paragraph{Operator meaning and counted arithmetic.}
A clocked minimum reduction, integer duplicate detection, and mixed type conversion do not map directly onto ordinary floating-point add/multiply/FMA counters. Their source purpose should be retained when interpreting absent scalar projections; absence of a projection establishes neither zero work nor a measured bottleneck.
\paragraph{Timer membership and sampled invocation.}
Output resets and guard fills precede the source timers, while warmup can invoke the same mathematical kernel as the later measured loop. For example, the contraction driver calls its closure before the ten-repeat timer. Source inclusion therefore does not identify which invocation a first-per-function/geometry capture sampled.
\paragraph{One operator, multiple mappings.}
The selected three-dimensional convolution evaluates one bounded operator with three grid-order layouts. CUDA.jl selects three explicit kernels; KA and JACC specialize one body by layout. The one-dimensional study additionally spans FP64, FP32 and integer paths. These mappings are not separate scientific families, and their arithmetic projections cannot be summed against a single peak.
