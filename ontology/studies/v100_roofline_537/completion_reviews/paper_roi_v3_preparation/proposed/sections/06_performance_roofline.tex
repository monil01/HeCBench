\subsection{RQ4: From descriptors to mechanisms}
A source motif suggests a hypothesis; it does not establish executed traffic. The constant-memory benchmark's native SASS exits nonwriters before loads, while a shared-memory swap probe has a permutation-invariant answer. Instruction/counter evidence is needed to establish their work. Floating-point rooflines require consistent work, measured traffic, duration and ceilings; integer, atomic and launch probes require other metrics.

Native CUDA, CUDA.jl and KA reach 128--131~GFLOP/s at 0.178 useful FLOP/DRAM byte. Default JACC reserves 49,152 dynamic shared bytes per block despite no shared algorithmic data, limiting the reported shared-memory occupancy to two blocks versus 32. Setting the reservation to zero preserves the body, inputs and geometry; all five output checks pass. The mean is 67.24~$\mu$s; a separate profile falls from 161.34 to 64.32~$\mu$s and reaches 130.42~GFLOP/s. A prescribed 17-configuration intervention also includes slowdowns, so this is a specific resource mechanism, not a universal explanation.

Two further controls delimit that conclusion. An unchanged interleave operator preserves both AoS and SoA layouts; across four versions and four fixtures, SoA is 8.49--8.78 times faster. Traffic was not profiled, so layout sensitivity does not prove a DRAM bottleneck. A Mat\'ern5/2 JACC shared-arena diagnostic reduces the mean from 1.727 to 0.187~ms with unchanged outputs, but also changes registers and local traffic; the 9.23-fold gain cannot be assigned solely to initialization bytes. That diagnostic is a CUDA-specific hybrid and remains secondary to the public API path.

\paragraph{Ontology-linked profiles and scope.}
The full-suite campaign remains incomplete. An explicit 537-name, four-version registry separates measured kernel geometry, no-counted-FP cases, source-reviewed runtime inapplicability and missing measurements. First-per-specialization samples can include warmup and unassigned stages. A stricter source-counted audit admits twelve complete selected scientific-family captures: Triad, STREAM dot and Jacobi stencil in all four versions (technical companion). Their integrated executed-work/DRAM coordinates preserve launch multiplicities; they are cold replay observations, separate from five-trial means and the useful-work intervention above. Numerically accepted Jacobi implementations execute different counted FP32 work, illustrating why a source motif alone cannot supply the numerator. Host-mapped data require host/PCIe ceilings; tensor conversions require calibrated factors and source-bound arithmetic review. Geometric position does not establish a causal bottleneck, and partial kernel samples cannot support a whole-benchmark intensity--speedup correlation.

Separate source-callpath reviews cover 242 callsite inclusion declarations across 150 selected model records and 39 names; aliases are not independent kernel families. Integer Hamming counts, CPU/GPU mixed elapsed sums and atomic payload transport illustrate why a benchmark name or data type cannot supply executed floating-point work. This source evidence adds no measured invocation or complete elapsed-ROI capture; the twelve strict scientific-family captures are unchanged.

The explanatory contribution is the chain from a scientific requirement to a recorded reduction, precision or memory-tier choice, an intervention and a bounded conclusion. The companion separates operational profiling failures from translation feedback, including selected observations, historical attempts and missing measurements. Family correlations alone do not supply that chain.
