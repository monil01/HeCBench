\paragraph{A mixture-model pipeline includes shared host decisions} The selected GMM source times the complete CPU convergence/model-order controller and GPU stages, subtracting explicit pre/post-stage observation intervals. Julia callbacks retain that host controller via C ABI and provide their own GPU stage dispatch. The seven source stage families do not establish a continuous elapsed-device interval, a complete sampled timed phase, or a complete estimator-quality result.

\paragraph{Graph execution changes the launch path, not the reduction identity} The same two-stage Float32-input/Float64-accumulator reduction segment is captured into a graph and also launched directly. The reported timer sums two paths with H2D, partial resets and D2H. A sampled reduction kernel cannot be assigned to timed graph replay versus stream execution without phase evidence; whole timer rates must not be inferred from its counters.

\paragraph{Half output is distinct from half arithmetic} The selected GRU cell computes sigmoid/tanh gates and intermediate combinations with Float32 arithmetic, projects outputs and five saved-state planes into Float16, and widens those arrays for the artifact outside the timer. Gate-affine inputs are already supplied: this does not time their matrix multiplications, a full recurrent network, or training. GPP separately uses Float64 split-complex reductions, illustrating why scientific stage and source arithmetic need distinct fields.
