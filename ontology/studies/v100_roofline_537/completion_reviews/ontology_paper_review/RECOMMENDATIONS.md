## Three empirical associations worth foregrounding

1. **Diagnostic/reduction policy ↔ counted operation mix (Jacobi).** The PDE-family source intent is twenty relaxation steps with all twenty normalized L2 update observations, not convergence. Native CUDA uses a block/shuffle reduction; the Julia implementations use per-point floating atomics. The source-counted audit reports 858.849760M versus 502.333920M ordinary FP32 operations, respectively. All versions have 502.333920M core field-and-square operations; the extra 356.515840M native adds implement its reduction tree. Floating atomic arithmetic is outside the selected ordinary add/multiply/FMA numerator. Therefore native's higher intensity is not evidence of additional physical work, more relaxation steps, or better efficiency. This connects an accuracy/diagnostic requirement to reduction policy and the metric boundary. It does not compare complete floating work or a converged PDE solve.

2. **Same counted operator ↔ different cold-replay traffic (STREAM dot).** All four selected dot families count 5,895,680 FP32 operations over ten launches. JACC reports 122,325,312 DRAM bytes versus native 20,989,760 (5.828x); integrated intensity is 0.0482 versus approximately 0.281. Triad has equal 838,860,800 counted operations and approximately 0.172 intensity across all four models. Thus traffic differences are case-specific, rather than evidence of a universal abstraction penalty. The JACC traffic's origin remains unresolved: no retained selected-instance SASS/local-memory proof establishes a spill or shared-memory cause. Warm/cold behavior, spill mechanisms and ordinary runtime impact require separately controlled measurements.

3. **Memory placement / backend boundary ↔ appropriate measurement question.** Source-reviewed zerocopy uses actual CPU-resident mapped allocations, not HBM arrays; HBM-only projections cannot describe its primary data path. Source-only eligibility can be shown now even though its selected ledger presently lacks counters. Source-reviewed runtime-only operations and integer/address workloads similarly require other views. A scientific family is not itself a guarantee of an FP roofline: the frozen descriptor's discrete/crypto family has zero names with eligible FP positions in each version, but that is not zero work and must not hide missing captures. Show no-counted-FP, inapplicable and unmeasured as distinct outcomes.

## Community framing / what selection guidance is justified

- For a PDE scientist: preserve the residual/update-norm semantics and tolerance before interpreting arithmetic intensity. A reduction replacement can change rounding and counted work while the field remains accepted.
- For developers of vector/reduction components: compare map and reduction families separately. The Triad counterexample rules out treating the observed STREAM traffic gap as a framework-wide constant. No framework recommendation follows from the gap alone.
- For mixed CPU/GPU scientific workflows: identify memory placement, vendor boundaries and the physical stage first. Scientific timesteps may include host diagnostics, transfers, communication and many library calls; authored kernel count is not the launch count or time fraction.
- Framework selection should therefore report the tested configuration, accuracy contract and implementation class. The separate controlled shared-memory Triad intervention supports checking launch-resource defaults in that case; the partial campaign cannot support 'PDE→KA' or 'ML→JACC' prescriptions.
